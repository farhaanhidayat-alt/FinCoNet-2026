General Information

Essential Information for Visiting Indonesia

About Nusa Dua
Nusa Dua is a secure and well-managed tourism complex in Bali, offering international-standard hotels, beaches, conference facilities, and cultural experiences. It is widely used for international meetings and high-level events.
Currency
The official currency is Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). Credit cards are widely accepted in hotels, restaurants, and major establishments. Participants are advised to carry some cash for small purchases.
Weather in December
Bali has a tropical climate. In December, temperatures generally range from Approx. 24°C to 32°C, with occasional rainfall. Light clothing and an umbrella or light raincoat are recommended.
Tipping
Tipping is not mandatory in Indonesia but is appreciated for good service. A tip of around 5–10% may be given where a service charge is not included.
Electricity
The standard voltage is 230V and the frequency is 50Hz. Plug types C and F are commonly used. Participants are encouraged to bring suitable travel adapters and chargers.
Time Zone
Bali observes Central Indonesia Time (WITA / GMT+8).
Emergency Number
General Emergency: 112 | Police: 110 | Ambulance: 118

Tourist Destinations in Bali

Explore iconic sights and cultural landmarks near Nusa Dua

1. Geger Beach

The closest and most relaxing beach near Apurva, offering soft golden sand, calm clear water, and peaceful morning coastal atmosphere.

2. Water Blow

A dramatic natural phenomenon where ocean waves crash against limestone cliffs, surging high into the air.

3. Peninsula Island

A serene green park surrounded by ocean panoramas and cliffside walking paths, perfect for sunset relaxation.

4. Pandawa Beach

Famed for its entry road carved through high limestone cliffs lined with giant hero statues and clear turquoise water.

5. Garuda Wisnu Kencana

A iconic cultural landmark with one of the world's tallest statues, traditional dances, and wide plaza views.

6. Puja Mandala

A unique religious complex demonstrating interfaith harmony with five distinct places of worship standing side-by-side.

Bali Culinary Destinations

Taste authentic flavors and world-class cuisine

1. Warung Halme Ikan Bakar

Fresh fish, prawns, squid, and shellfish grilled over coconut charcoal with Balinese sambals at local-warung prices.

2. Jaansan Bali Restaurant

Sample authentic Balinese dishes like ayam betutu, sate lilit, and local seafood in a welcoming environment.

3. Raja Bali

Traditional Balinese recipes, slow-cooked meats, and ceremonial-style dishes reflecting Bali's culinary heritage.

4. Kenja Ikan Bakar & Seafood

Relaxed setting in Nusa Dua specializing in grilled fish and shellfish prepared with Indonesian seasonings.

5. Arwana Restaurant

Oceanfront fine dining known for premium seafood and stunning sunset views right beside the beach.

6. Koral Aquarium Restaurant

Bali's first aquarium restaurant located inside The Apurva Kempinski, offering a fine-dining tasting menu beside a large aquarium.

Bali Spa & Relaxation Destinations

Rejuvenate with traditional treatments

1. The Apurva Spa

The most convenient spa is, of course, The Apurva Spa, located within the resort itself.

2. Rosehill Spa

Rosehill spa is literally located between Hilton Bali Resort and Apurva, making it one of the nearest independent spas available.

3. Karma Spa Therapy & Wellness

Focuses on wellness-oriented treatments rather than just relaxation, with a healing-centered approach.

4. Zahra Luxury Spa Nusa Dua

Combines luxury-style treatment rooms with affordable pricing compared to five-star hotel spas.

5. La Nusa Bali Spa

Especially popular among couples, with an emphasis on romantic spa experiences and Balinese massage rituals.

6. Royal Orchid Spa

A favorite for traditional Balinese massage combining aromatherapy and body treatments in a quiet, intimate environment.

Bali Gift Shops & Souvenirs

Find authentic local crafts and memories

1. Bali Collection

The most popular shopping destination near Apurva, featuring an open-air lifestyle complex filled with souvenir shops, handicraft boutiques, and fashion stores.

2. Aget Collection

One of the best-rated specialty souvenir shops in Nusa Dua for authentic Balinese souvenirs and curated handicrafts.

3. Bali In Bali

Located inside Bali Collection, specializing in traditional Balinese arts, wood carvings, and cultural keepsakes.

4. Skala Living

A boutique focused on contemporary Indonesian design, artisan-made lifestyle products, and beautiful home décor.

5. Island Jewels

Specializes in premium jewelry inspired by Bali's natural beauty and culture, perfect for lasting personal mementos.

6. Sensatia – Bali Collection

Known for natural skincare, soaps, essential oils, and wellness products made locally in Bali.

7. Jemba Jemba Souvenir

A smaller, local-style shop inside Bali Collection offering classic Bali gifts like magnets, keychains, and sarongs.

8. Krisna

Bali's most popular souvenir shop offering a wide range of local gifts, conveniently located near the airport for last-minute shopping.
